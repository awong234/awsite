---
title: Overboard
date: 2026-05-15
dream_tags: [boats, underwater, drowning]
---

I dreamt that I was part of administration on a sea vessel and I took sick days
for my ailment. Maybe me and a few other officers it seemed like.

There’s a cultural event where we set prisoners free, but its mainly for show
because they dump coffins overboard all chained up and then go unlock them.

The captain seems to have disliked our sick day usage because she made us
prisoners, having grumbled on about productivity numbers, but seems to have
forgotten about the event that would release us. She’s reminded, almost lectured
on decorum by someone else. Grudgingly she accedes.

I’m locked in a coffin alongside others. We’re assured that the unlocking never
goes wrong.

The coffin chain is dumped overboard, and in the moment I feel I have lots of
time to hyperventilate and hold breath before hitting water.

Underwater, in dreaming fashion, I am at no risk of actually drowning, but like
you might do when watching a movie I hold my breath to see if I could make it
back to the surface. I awake gasping.
