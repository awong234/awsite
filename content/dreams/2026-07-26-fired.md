Progressive x star wars/disney?

At work, most faces I don’t recognize. We’re doing some sort of retrospective
but as a department.

We’re writing down our sprints like diaries on a sheet of paper for the month.
I’m missing some events towards the end of the month but thats really just
because I got a little lazy writing everything down. I figured my work would
speak for itself.

They’re huge sticklers for the rules though. I get in trouble and nobody is
trying to help me out of this small mistake. Theyre trying to get me to speak
about my last sprint but i find it difficult. I receive my final review and I’ve
gotten a 92 grade; just 4 points shy of failure. But, it turns out, I got a 2/4
Force points and this is enough to send me packing. I’m devastated. I crumple
and some teammates try to comfort me, including Ho-Min. I awake crying.
