On a trip for work. Its between a shore and an island. Have to take a ferry
around.

There’s some weird fighting activity where we have to sneak up on something and
another team has to defend it. I’m not too into it.

One guy keeps following me around, even though I try to lose him on the
elevator.

Ludia is on the island. Maybe part of another group. She starts throwing cats at
me, all black cats, as a joke. I’m weirded out; I dont have to be on the island
so I decide to try and get back to shore. I think I can swim it so I jump off
the ferry, a big dive, but someone says I might hit the propeller so I cancel it
and wait till we get closer to shore, I can just jump off directly.

I see another team in misery, I guess they’re signing some sort of legal
documents.
