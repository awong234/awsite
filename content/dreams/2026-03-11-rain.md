House with vaulted ceiling like a cathedral, many stories high. Rain starts
coming down but the roof is in disrepair so water is pouring into the house,
onto our bed. I try to cover the bed with more covers hoping the rain would wick
off onto the floor. I wasnt worried about the floor for some reason.

I went out back to try to find binoculars in our storage shed. Everything was
muddy and flooded. We had a small wooded area where the shed was, and then a
little higher in elevation what appeared to be a small plot of cropland.
