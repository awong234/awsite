---
title: Nuclear Option
date: 2026-03-22
dream_tags: [cataclysm]
---

Me and three acquaintances were given a vote on whether to allow the world to
end by nuke or continue on. For some reason I voted to end it.

I expected a swift end. But instead what happened was the world turned bitterly,
dangerously cold. If we didn’t find fire quickly we would die. We moved from
spot to spot trying to find open fires.
