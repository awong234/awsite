standard syntax, but then they bust out a syntax that looks really fricken
crazy, like pictographs of air-to-air combat, bullet trails, parachutes, etc.
I’m looking at it like wtf is this? It’s a new package apparently. Is this how
people felt about dplyr?

A skunk skunks in real life but I haven’t woken up just yet. I smell it in the
dream and get up to leave the classroom, apologizing to the instructor by
mouthing the word “sorry” and I go outside for fresh air.
