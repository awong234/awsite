I'm either a corporate spy or a government spy. It’s set in the star wars
universe.

I’m tasked with secretly boarding a tiny module of a ship to learn its
capabilities. I’m supposed to be locked in there for maybe days while the module
is being tested.

I dont want to see all of that so I fast forward to the part I get out. I’m in
an experimental landscape, one where every single type of water body is
represented in sections, as if a botanic garden curated and marked the types.

I am in the section where waves near shore convalesce into a larger wave. I walk
upland and follow the water to a source farther inland. I’m in a forested place
where pristine water runs over grass in varying amounts. In one section, I look
up and I see a great old forest (think Fangorn) but the trees are in rows so you
can see very far in. It’s hazy and mysterious looking down a row of trees, and
all their giant boughs are raised and intertwined as if they were all fighting.
The water here is purest.
