title: Flight End of Vacation
date: 2026-05-07
dream_tags: [London, animals, gruesome, airplane]
---

Leaving a place we went for vacation. We were maybe in london. I remember
finding in my wallet a card from a hotel that showed i stayed in London in 2017
(irl closest was scotland in 2018).

I remember seeing a little pond with cabins around it. As we were in the air
going home I saw it from overhead, thinking it would have been nice to stay
there.

The aircraft is very small and I have a large field of vision outside the
window, so much so its like I’m outside. I watch as we pass by some birds; my
seat neighbor or maybe one behind me thinks he spotted an owl.

We’re flying low, and there are a lot of birds flying around. Suddenly there is
a goose or something large sucked into the engine. What is spit out is a
mostly-intact bird like what you’d buy at the grocery store, except with more
feathers. It kept sailing along with us even though it was clearly dead. I
thought how horrid air travel was.
