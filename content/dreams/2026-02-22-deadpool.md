him, try to make jokes and stuff.

Ascending a giant ship to the top. I was acutely aware it was a game because I
knew if I died I would reload, but I didn’t want the sensation of dying. I
carefully made my way up past a conveyor belt full of ice to be dumped into an
industrial shredder.

I was fiddling with my kit, wasnt sure how best to store my katanas and guns.
The katanas for some reason could retract into the handles.

Met up with some other deadpools at the top. Made some meta aware jokes to the
person in charge, her name was darrah or something.
