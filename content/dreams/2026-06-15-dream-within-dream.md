I dreamt I was telling my cousin katie about a dream that I shared — with her
sister sammy — about Katie.

I was telling her that her family was all there, including ania. Her family was
really mean, with the exception of katie and sammy. I had somehow some way
discovered thet sammy had the same dream.

I was eager to show Katie my record — I had made tons of notes on an old
newspaper. The newspaper itself factored into the dream-within-a-dream. For
example there was a ring that Katie was supposed to have owned, and it would
have opened a door or revealed something, but katie didn’t wear it that day and
we couldnt locate a picture of it for katie to remember where it was.

There was a portion of the dream where we were in a cave-like structure but it
was behind some apartments. These apartments were in the dream-within-a-dream. I
was trying to put a listening device near one of the apartments to prove what I
knew from the inner dream. Some woman in a bath robe walked out onto a ledge
while I was doing this; I ignored her and acted like nothing was unusual.

I’ll not sure if related: at a sea-world type place. There are giant pacific
octopuses being moved from one tank to another.
