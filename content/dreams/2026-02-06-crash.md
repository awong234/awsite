Lisa and I parking the car at home, but not our house. We exit the car thinking
the job is done. We didn’t put it in park, we didn’t even shut off the car. The
car jumps backwards in reverse as if the throttle was pressed all the way down,
we’re scrambling to try and get back safely into this lurching vehicle.
